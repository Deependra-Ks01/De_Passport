Blockchain-Based Digital Passport System

Issue and verify passports with MongoDB, Ganache, and Web3.py.

Prototype portal for a passport authority, passport holder, and verifier. Passport records stay in MongoDB while the blockchain stores only the document hash and validity state.

Admin Login

Authority

Issue and revoke passport records, then anchor each document hash on Ethereum.

Citizen

Look up a passport using the wallet address and generate a QR code for sharing.

Verifier

Paste a QR payload to validate the passport against MongoDB and the blockchain.
